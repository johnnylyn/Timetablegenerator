Renames teachers whose ID matches 1–37 to the current placeholder names, in place — your curriculum, availability, and generated timetable are untouched since they're linked by ID, not name.

The generator uses a randomized backtracking placer: it respects "no teacher in two places at once", "one appearance of a subject per class per day" (except the two halves of a double), and "a double period must sit in one unbroken block". It also nudges free periods away from period 1–2 where possible. It's a strong first pass, not a guarantee of a perfect fit — check the warnings list after generating, and adjust any cell by hand.
